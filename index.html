09 / FAQ

Before you
arrive.

Everything you need to know about registration, eligibility, events, schedule, and participation.

Who can register and can a single person (solo) enter?

Any college student across India can register regardless of branch or year. Yes, solo entries (1 person) are fully allowed across all tracks, including the 3-day Hackathon, Idea Pitching, and MUN. You can compete individually or as a team of up to 6 members.

What are the official dates and event durations?

E-Summit 2026 takes place over three days from September 26 to September 28, 2026 at DIT University, Dehradun. The Hackathon is a flagship 3-day continuous sprint (Sep 26–28). Other events like workshops, pitching rounds, MUN, and seminars are 1-day structured tracks scheduled across the 3 days.

Can I participate in multiple events?

Yes, as long as the events don't overlap in the schedule. You'll select a primary track during registration but can attend open sessions and workshops across tracks.

What should I bring to the 3-day hackathon?

Your laptop, charger, and any hardware you plan to use. Wi-Fi, power, meals, workspace, and mentorship will be provided throughout the 3-day duration (Sep 26–28). All code must be written on-site — no pre-built projects.

How does idea pitching work?

Submit a one-page concept brief when you register. If shortlisted, you'll present a 5-minute pitch followed by Q&A. Finalists pitch on Day 3 (28 Sep). Prizes include incubation support from CIIES and mentor introductions.

Will accommodation be provided?

Accommodation details for outstation participants will be shared upon registration confirmation. DIT University campus facilities will be allocated on a first-come basis.

Can I attend without competing?

Yes. Seminars, workshops, the talent showcase, and networking sessions are open to all registered attendees regardless of competition participation.

Still have a question?

Reach out to the organizing team at CIIES and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
